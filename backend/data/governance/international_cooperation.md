# Multilateral AI Safety Coordination and Verification Treaty

## 1. Preamble and Treaty Objective

The Signatory States to this Treaty, recognizing the mutual perils posed by uncoordinated AI governance and the destabilizing consequences of competitive race dynamics, hereby establish permanent mechanisms for diplomatic consultation, joint algorithmic verification, and peaceful resolution of AI-related interstate disputes.

## 2. Institutional Architecture

### Article 2.1: The International AI Governance Council (IAIGC)
The IAIGC shall serve as the primary deliberative body, convened biannually and on an emergency basis within 6 hours of any Tier 3/4 incident. Each signatory nation maintains one designated delegate.

### Article 2.2: The Multilateral Verification Inspectorate (MVI)
A technically non-partisan agency empowered to conduct on-site inspections, evaluate model weights in secure clean rooms, verify zero-knowledge proofs of alignment compliance, and audit high-risk computing facilities.

## 3. Verification and Inspection Procedures

1. **Routine Audits**: Facilities operating compute clusters exceeding $10^{26}$ FLOPs shall submit quarterly attestation reports verifying hardware root-of-trust, physical tamper-proofing, and alignment training regimens.
2. **Challenge Inspections**: Any signatory state presenting credible prima facie evidence of non-compliant autonomous agent deployment may petition the Council for a Challenge Inspection.
3. **Zero-Knowledge Auditing**: To protect legitimate commercial trade secrets and sovereign defense secrets, the MVI shall utilize zero-knowledge cryptographic proofs, enabling mathematical verification of safety constraints without disclosing proprietary model architecture or raw source weights.

## 4. Voting Rules and Consensus Thresholds

- **Emergency Action Resolutions**: Approving coordinated emergency interventions (e.g. mandatory international network quarantines or global algorithmic trading circuit-breakers) requires a qualified majority of 60% of participating states, including at least one representative from each major geopolitical bloc.
- **Sanctions and Redline Enforcement**: Imposing collective economic or technological sanctions for verified treaty non-compliance requires a two-thirds supermajority vote of the Council.
- **Ordinary Procedural Motions**: Handled via simple majority (50% + 1).

## 5. Dispute Settlement and Arbitration

1. In the event of interstate disputes regarding AI incident attribution, economic damage liability, or algorithmic espionage, parties agree to submit to the jurisdiction of the International AI Arbitration Tribunal (IAAT) hosted in neutral sovereign territory.
2. Decisions rendered by the Tribunal are final, binding, and enforceable through multilateral trade and clearing mechanisms.

## 6. Sovereign Non-Interference Guarantees

Inspectors and Council resolutions must refrain from seeking, disclosing, or weaponizing proprietary military doctrine, domestic intelligence methods, or commercial intellectual property unrelated to verified safety risks.
